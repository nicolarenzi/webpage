Čuoikkariššu / Mosquito Shower

Čuoikkariššu / Mosquito Shower is a sound installation exploring climate microchanges through the buzzing presence of Arctic mosquitoes…

Did you know?
  • Mosquito wingbeat frequency varies by species, sex, and temperature.
  • Pairs can adjust wingbeat tones to “match” each other during courtship.
  • What we hear as a buzz is largely the wingbeat plus harmonics.
